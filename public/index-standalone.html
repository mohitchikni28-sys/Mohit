The Story Behind Himalayan Flames

Founded with a passion to bring true mountain-style momos to Ahmedabad, Himalayan Flames House of Momo stands out with traditional dough craftsmanship, freshly prepared savory stuffing, and slow-cooked spice pastes.

Unlike commercial frozen snacks, every dumpling is pinched by hand right here. From our velvety sesame-tomato Jhol Momos to the golden crunchy Peri-Peri Kurkure Momos, we preserve pure Himalayan street soul in every bite.
